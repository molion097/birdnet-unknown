# Conservation status and withheld coordinates

The IUCN Red List category of all 847 species in the four benchmarks was looked up on 28 September 2026
through GBIF's Red List mirror (`https://api.gbif.org/v1/species/{key}/iucnRedListCategory`). Every species
returned a GBIF match and a Red List category, and all withheld species matched under their exact benchmark
name. A repeat check on 12 October 2026 gave the same category for every species.

For the 136 species assessed Near Threatened or worse, the coordinates of all their recordings are removed
from the released tables. All other coordinates are rounded to 0.1 degree. Not Evaluated and Data Deficient
species are not withheld, because these categories are not threat categories.
